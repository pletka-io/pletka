What does Pletka mean?

<!--key:pages.home.pletka_meaning_text-->
Pletka takes its name from the Bulgarian word for **weaving** — a traditional knowledge practice of interlacing threads into functional and meaningful patterns. Inspired by this craft, Pletka approaches semantic data in the same way: as weaving classes and properties together into reusable structures, shared understanding, and evolving knowledge systems. The platform is designed as a library for managing semantic patterns that can be composed, adapted, and connected across contexts.
